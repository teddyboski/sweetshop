## Long / full description (both stores, 4000 characters max)

Snacks that hit different.

The Sweet Shop started live on Whatnot, hand-packing snack boxes on
camera for people who love snacks as much as we do. We never stopped —
we just added an app.

**What's inside:**

- Curated snack boxes, hand-packed fresh to order — never sitting in a
  warehouse
- Build-your-own box — pick your own mix, no two boxes the same
- Limited-time drops — mystery and collab boxes that sell out fast, with
  push alerts the moment they go live
- A monthly subscription box, if you want your snack fix on autopilot
- Order tracking, right in the app, from "packed" to "on your porch"
- Rewards points on every order, redeemable for discounts
- Refer a friend, you both get rewarded

Secure checkout with Apple Pay and Google Pay. Real support from a real
snack-obsessed team — not a bot.

Follow @sweet_shop_official on Whatnot to catch us live.
